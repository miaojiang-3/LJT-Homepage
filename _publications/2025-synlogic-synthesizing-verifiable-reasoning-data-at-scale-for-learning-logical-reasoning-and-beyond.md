SynLogic proposes a framework for synthesizing large-scale verifiable reasoning data to improve logical reasoning abilities in large language models. By leveraging logical puzzles and datasets, it enables models to learn systematic reasoning through verifiable supervision, with applications beyond logical reasoning tasks.

*First-authored by Junteng Liu; published on arXiv 2025.*
