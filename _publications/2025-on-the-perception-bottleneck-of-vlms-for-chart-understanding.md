This paper analyzes the perception bottleneck in vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding tasks. Through a series of controlled experiments, it identifies where VLM architectures fail to perceive critical information in charts, and proposes analyses to better understand and potentially mitigate this limitation.

*First-authored by Junteng Liu; published on arXiv 2025.*
