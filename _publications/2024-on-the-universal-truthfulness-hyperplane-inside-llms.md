This paper studies the universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models. It finds that truthfulness in LLMs can be characterized by a separating hyperplane in the hidden representation space, providing insights into how models distinguish truthful from untruthful outputs and suggesting methods for improving factuality.

*First-authored by Junteng Liu; published at EMNLP 2024.*
