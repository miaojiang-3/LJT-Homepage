This work investigates the sharpness of inner representations as an indicator for hallucination detection. By analyzing representational sharpness during in-context learning, the authors show that a sharp representation correlates with the model's tendency to produce hallucinations, offering an inner-representation perspective for mitigating such issues.

*Co-authored by Junteng Liu; published at ICML 2024.*
