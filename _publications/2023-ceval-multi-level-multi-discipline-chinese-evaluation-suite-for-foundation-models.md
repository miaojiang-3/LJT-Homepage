C-Eval is a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate the reasoning abilities of large language models across multiple disciplines. It contains 14,600 questions spanning 26 disciplines and four difficulty levels, providing a robust and reliable measure of Chinese LLM capabilities.

*Co-authored by Junteng Liu; published at NeurIPS 2023.*
