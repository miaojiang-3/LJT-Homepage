# Junteng Liu

**PhD Candidate | HKUST NLP Group**

First-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, affiliated with the HKUST NLP Group led by Professor Junxian He.

## Biography

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at HKUST, pursuing research in Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning. His work focuses on large language models (LLMs), including LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability. During his undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, he also worked as a research intern at Shanghai AI Lab under Prof. Yu Cheng before joining the HKUST NLP Group as a PhD student.

## Academic Background

Junteng Liu earned his B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, graduating in June 2024 with the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. He then began his PhD studies at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2024, working within the HKUST NLP Group under the supervision of Professor Junxian He, who also served as his advisor during his undergraduate years.

He also completed a research internship at Shanghai AI Lab from June 2023 to December 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Yu Cheng. This experience contributed to his early research interests in machine learning and natural language processing before transitioning to the HKUST NLP Group.
